# Public pilot

## User problem

People who depend on old X posts cannot reliably rediscover them through the live service. The official archive is machine-readable but cumbersome to inspect.

## Product promise

Open an official archive locally and find a past post by word, date or post type without uploading the archive.

## Signals

- real users
- users who open an archive
- users who perform a search
- users who export a filtered result
- users who explicitly save and later reopen a local copy
- returning users on another JST day

Automated QA is excluded. Content, search terms, file names and result counts are never recorded.

## Initial decision rule

Review after at least 30 days. Continue product investment when at least five real users open archives, at least three search, and at least two export or reopen a saved local copy. Hold if fewer than two users open an archive or no user searches.
